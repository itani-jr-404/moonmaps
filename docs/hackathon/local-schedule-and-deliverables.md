# Local schedule and deliverables

Last updated: 2026-11-14.

What the Beirut event asks of us, and when. All times are Beirut time.

Source: four slides shown by the NASA Space Apps Beirut organizers (the 2026 schedule, "Needed tasks for the hackathon", "Get ready to submit", and the judging criteria), photographed by the team on 2026-11-14. The organizers' site is spaceappsbeirut.com. If the organizers announce a change, it overrides this file.

## What this means for the build

- The app has to be ready to demonstrate by **Sunday 3:00 PM**, not by the midnight deadline.
- The citations and the documentation are judged, which is why the repository's two standing rules are to cite everything and to keep the documentation current.
- The current plan against these times is in [team/BUILD-PLAN.md](../../team/BUILD-PLAN.md).
