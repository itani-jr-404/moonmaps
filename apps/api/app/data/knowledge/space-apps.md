# NASA Space Apps Challenge and the Beirut event

Last updated: 2026-11-15.

## What NASA Space Apps is

The NASA Space Apps Challenge is a hackathon held every year. It offers students, technologists and space science fans a way to use NASA open data to respond to science and space exploration challenges. It is open to all ages and skill levels, and it is free; anyone asked to pay should report it to info@spaceappschallenge.org [2].

It takes place at hundreds of events around the world. Each Local Event is organised by a Local Lead. People with no event nearby can join the virtual Universal Event [2]. NASA Space Apps is funded by NASA's Science Mission Directorate through a contract with Booz Allen Hamilton, Mindgrub and SecondMuse [1]. The organisers describe it as a collaborative event, not a competitive one [5].

## The 2026 edition

- Theme: "The Next Frontier" [2].
- Dates: November 14-15, 2026 [3].
- Teams should have no more than six participants [3].
- Project submission opens Saturday, November 14 at 9:00 AM and closes Sunday, November 15 at 11:59 PM local time [3].
- There are 14 challenges [6] and 17 Space Agency Partners, among them the Canadian Space Agency [7].
- To be eligible for a Global Award, a project must use at least one NASA data source or resource [4].
- Projects are scored from 1 to 5 on Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance and Presentation, at every level of judging [5].
- Global Nominees are announced on December 3, 2026, Global Finalists and Honorable Mentions on December 17, 2026, and Global Winners on January 26, 2027 [5].
- Every participant who submits a project by the deadline receives a certificate of participation [5].
