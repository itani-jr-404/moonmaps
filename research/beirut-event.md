What the Beirut event asks for, on top of the global submission:

- 30-second video (for the global submission)
- 2-minute video (for local judging)
- PowerPoint presentation (for local judging or submission)
- Research and work posted on Slack (for local judging)
- Citations (for local judging)
- Project sample or solution on Google Drive or GitHub
- Documentation explaining the project and solution

Local judging criteria match the global ones (Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation). Under Relevance the local slide adds: "If AI is used, does it meaningfully support the project's goals?"
