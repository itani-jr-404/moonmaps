## **NASA Space Apps
Challenge Awards**
NASA Space Apps recognizes exceptional projects each year through its 10 Global Awards. The winning teams are determined by an executive committee of NASA and Space Agency Partner Subject Matter Experts.
[image: Award - Best Use of Science]

### Award

## Best Use of Science
The project that makes the best and most valid use of science and/or the scientific method.
[image: Award - Best Use of Data]

### Award

## Best Use of Data
The project that best makes space data accessible or leverages it for a unique application.
[image: Award - Best Use of Technology]

### Award

## Best Use of Technology
The project that exemplifies the most innovative use of technology.
[image: Award - Galactic Impact]

### Award

## Galactic Impact
The project with the most potential to improve life on Earth or in the universe.
[image: Award - Best Mission Concept]

### Award

## Best Mission Concept
The project with the most plausible concept and design.
[image: Award - Most Inspirational]

### Award

## Most Inspirational
The project that captures our hearts.
[image: Award - Best Use of Storytelling]

### Award

## Best Use of Storytelling
The project that most creatively communicates the potential of open data through the art of storytelling.
[image: Award - Global Connection]

### Award

## Global Connection
The project that best connects people around the world through technology.
[image: Award - Art & Technology]

### Award

## Art & Technology
The project that most effectively combines technical and creative skills.
[image: Award - Local Impact]

### Award

## Local Impact
The project that demonstrates the greatest potential for local impact.
[image: Awards02]

#### Global Nominees
Following the hackathon, Local Event and Universal Event judges will determine the projects that will advance to Global Judging, known as Global Nominees.
[image: Awards02]

#### Global Finalists
During Global Judging, all Global Nominees will be reviewed by NASA and Space Agency Partner Subject Matter Experts to determine the Global Finalists and Honorable Mentions.
[image: Awards02]

#### Global Winners
The Global Finalists will be reviewed by an executive committee from NASA and Space Agency Partners to determine 10 Global Winners. These judges are agency leaders and executives with a variety of subject matter expertise.
Discover the projects and teams recognized as 2025 NASA Space Apps Challenge [Global Nominees](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2025/awards/global-nominees/), [Global Finalists](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2025/awards/global-finalists/), and [Global Winners](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2025/awards/global-winners/).
