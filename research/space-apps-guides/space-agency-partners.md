## 2026 Space Agency Partners
NASA Space Apps is proud to partner with 17 Space Agency Partners from around the world to foster international collaboration by connecting cities and countries around the globe during the NASA Space Apps Challenge. Alongside NASA’s free and open data, Space Agency Partners provide additional spaced-based data for participants to use. These partnerships reflect the global nature of the NASA Space Apps Challenge and the collaborative spirit that drives innovation.
Our 2026 Space Agency Partners are:
[image: The logo for the Angola National Space Programme Management Office are the letters GGPEN in blue with a satellite circling it.]

#### Angola - National Space Programme Management Office
The Angola National Space Programme Management Office (GGPEN) was created with a simple belief: space technology should serve people. From operating the ANGOSAT-2 satellite that connects communities across Southern Africa, to using Earth observation data to support key sectors of Angola's economy, GGPEN builds space capability that solves real problems on the ground. As the host of the SADC Regional Satellite Programme and a signatory of the Artemis Accords, Angola is committed to a future where
[Learn More](https://www.ggpen.gov.ao/)
[image: The logo for the Argentina Space Agency features the bold black letters “CONAE” inside the outline of a blue circle.]

#### Argentina - National Commission on Space Activities
The National Commission on Space Activities of Argentina (CONAE) is the agency responsible for implementing the national space program, promoting and carrying out space activities for peaceful purposes across the productive, scientific, and educational sectors, and fostering the development of innovative national technologies throughout Argentina. CONAE recognizes the importance of space outreach and capacity building and has supported the NASA Space Apps Challenge since 2021.
[Learn More](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/ciencia/conae)
[image: The logo for the Bahrain Space Agency features a red and gold swoosh orbiting a blue circle with a small satellite above it.]

#### Bahrain Space Agency
The Bahrain Space Agency (BSA) aims to position the Kingdom of Bahrain as a global player in space science to support sustainable development. BSA focuses on space technologies for national growth, promoting space research and applications, and building local capacity. The agency collaborates and partners with government entities, the private sector, and universities.
[Learn More](https://bsa.gov.bh/)
[image: The logo for the Brazilian Space Agency features a blue sphere with a yellow orbital swoosh alongside bold blue letters reading AEB.]

#### Brazilian Space Agency
The Brazilian Space Agency (AEB) promotes the development of space activities to drive scientific advancement, technological innovation, and sustainable development. As a proud supporter of the NASA Space Apps Challenge, AEB values global collaboration and open innovation to engage new generations in addressing real-world challenges.
[Learn More](https://www.gov.br/aeb)
[image: The Canadian Space Agency logo has a red maple leaf with 3 read stars underneath it. At the bottom is the Canadian flag and the agency's name in English and French.]

#### Canadian Space Agency
The Canadian Space Agency (CSA) promotes the peaceful use and development of space through exploration, innovation, observation, and inspiration. Proudly supporting the NASA Space Apps Challenge since 2016, the CSA believes in the transformative power of open space data, uniting innovators to tackle challenges both on Earth and throughout the universe.
[Learn More](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/)
[image: The logo for the European Space Agency features a dark blue circular symbol with a small dot and the letter “e” inside, with the lowercase letters “esa” in matching dark blue to the right.]

#### European Space Agency
The European Space Agency (ESA) is Europe’s gateway to space. Its mission is to shape the development of Europe’s space capability and ensure that investment in space continues to deliver benefits to the citizens of Europe and the world. ESA strives to make its data and discoveries accessible to all, providing access to Earth Observation tools & science mission data.
[Learn More](https://esa.int/)
[image: The logo for the India Space Agency features an orange upward‑pointing arrow with two blue satellite‑like arms on either side of the arrow, with the agency name underneath.]

#### Indian Space Research Organization
The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) is the space agency of India. The organisation is involved in science, engineering and technology to harvest the benefits of outer space for India and mankind. ISRO is a major constituent of the Department of Space (DOS), Government of India.
[Learn More](https://www.isro.gov.in/)
[image: The logo for the Italian Space Agency features the letters “ASI” in dark blue with a sweeping arc forming a partial blue orbit around them, with the agency name written in Italian below.]

#### Italian Space Agency
The Italian Space Agency (ASI) is dedicated to promoting, coordinating, and advancing the national activities and contributions to the global space agenda, with a strong focus on scientific research, Earth observation, space exploration, and technological innovation. ASI believes in the power of open science and the role of global collaboration for our planet and beyond.
[Learn More](https://www.asi.it/)
[image: The logo for the Japan Space Agency features the stylized blue letters “JAXA,” with a sharp intersecting line forming an abstract star shape for the first “A.”]

#### Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency
The Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA) promotes Earth observation, data analysis, and application research as a core agency to develop Earth observation satellites in Japan. By monitoring the Earth's environment from space, JAXA aims to contribute to addressing global challenges.
[Learn More](https://earth.jaxa.jp/en/about/index.html)
[image: The logo for the South Korean Space Agency KASA has their letters with a red upward line for the top of the K culminating with a star over the first A.]

#### Korea AeroSpace Administration
The Korea AeroSpace Administration (KASA) is the national aerospace agency of the Republic of Korea and the control tower for the nation's space, exploration, and aeronautics endeavors. Its mission is to secure innovative technologies through the development of aerospace technology, promote the aerospace industry, and implement policies and projects to protect the public from space hazards.
[Learn More](https://www.kasa.go.kr/eng/index.do)
[image: The logo for the Nigerian National Space Research and Development Agency features a blue circle with an upward‑facing rocket in the center, and a diamond beneath it with three white stars on each side.]

#### Nigeria - National Space Research and Development Agency
Nigeria's Space Agency Mandate is to vigorously pursue the attainment of space capabilities and utilize space science and technology for sustainable national development and to improve the general quality of life in Nigeria.
[Learn More](https://central.nasrda.gov.ng/)
[image: The logo for the Paraguay Space Agency features a yellow star framed by red and blue branches, with the agency name written beside it.]

#### Paraguayan Space Agency
The Paraguayan Space Agency (AEP) is an autonomous entity under the administrative and functional authority of the Presidency of the Republic as established by law. Its mission is to lead, design, and implement national policies and programs in the field of space and aerospace. As a proud supporter of the NASA Space Apps Challenge, the Paraguayan Space Agency champions youth participation, open innovation, and international collaboration to unlock the potential of space for national progress.
[Learn More](https://aep.gov.py/)
[image: The logo for the Senegalese Space Studies Agency features a shooting star above a black‑and‑white Earth, with the star’s green, yellow, and red striped tail streaking across a blue sky.]

#### Senegalese Space Studies Agency
The Senegalese Space Studies Agency (ASES) is the national agency responsible for developing and implementing Senegal's national policy and strategy for the study, research, training, promotion and development of space activities.
[Learn More](https://sites.google.com/view/redirectionases/ases?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
[image: The logo for the Spain Space Agency features the stylized blue letters “AEE” with curved extensions forming a sleek, aerodynamic shape, with the agency name written below.]

#### Spanish Space Agency
The Spanish Space Agency (AEE) believes that space has the power to improve our lives, protect our planet, and spark curiosity and innovation. That’s why they are proud to support the NASA Space Apps Challenge, a global hackathon that brings people together to address real-world problems using creativity, science, and teamwork. The Spanish Space Agency works to make space part of everyone’s future—by promoting research, supporting innovation, and encouraging young minds to explore new ideas.
[Learn More](https://www.aee.gob.es/)
[image: The logo for the Turkish Space Agency features the bold red letters “TUA,” with the center of the “A” forming a white star, and the agency name written below.]

#### Turkish Space Agency
The Turkish Space Agency (TUA) is a state authority which carries out the activities on law, management, etc. to support the development & dissemination of space, aeronautics sciences & technologies, & coordinates the work within the national & international organizations to protect & safeguard the space-related rights & interests of the Republic of Türkiye.
[Learn More](https://tua.gov.tr/en)
[image: The logo for the Mohammed Bin Rashid Space Centre features a curved blue arc above Arabic text, with the center’s name written in English underneath.]

#### United Arab Emirates - Mohammed Bin Rashid Space Centre
The Mohammed Bin Rashid Space Centre (MBRSC) is a leading hub for scientific innovation and space exploration in the UAE, driving missions in Earth observation, human spaceflight, & lunar exploration. Their support of NASA Space Apps reflects their commitment to foster youth engagement, research, & innovation.
[Learn More](http://www.mbrsc.ae/)
[image: The logo for the UK Space Agency features a red arrow with flag‑like stripes and the agency name to the right in red and blue.]

#### United Kingdom Space Agency
The UK Space Agency is a part of the UK's Department of Business, Science, Innovation and Trade. We work hard to catalyse investment, deliver missions and capabilities, and champion space. As a proud supporter of the NASA Space Apps Challenge, the UK Space Agency believes in the power of space-derived data and capabilities to elevate innovation to new heights, expand global growth and collaboration, and enrich the lives of all citizens on Earth.
[Learn More](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/uk-space-agency)
