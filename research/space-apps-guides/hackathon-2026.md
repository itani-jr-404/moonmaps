## **Welcome to the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge!**
Join us and explore *The Next Frontier* on November 14-15.
Step 01

## CREATE ACCOUNT
August 26, 2026
The first step in your hackathon journey is to create a NASA Space Apps account or log in to your existing account.
Step 02

## REGISTER FOR THE HACKATHON
August 26, 2026
To participate and be eligible for Global Judging, you must first register for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge. You can register up until the hackathon closes on November 15.
Step 03

## CHOOSE A LOCAL EVENT
August 26, 2026
Connect with the NASA Space Apps community by choosing an in-person or virtual Local Event near you. If you don’t have a Local Event near you, choose the virtual Universal Event. You can choose or change your event up until the hackathon closes on November 15.
[Choose a Local Event](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/local-events/)
Step 04

## JOIN OR FORM A TEAM
September 17, 2026
Collaborate with participants by forming or joining a team according to your chosen challenge. Teams should have no more than six participants. You can choose or change your team up until the hackathon closes on November 15.
[Browse Teams](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/find-a-team/)
Step 05

## SUBMIT A PROJECT
November 15, 2026
To edit and submit your project, go to the Project Tab on your Team’s page. Project submission opens on Saturday, November 14 at 9:00 AM and closes on Sunday, November 15 at 11:59 PM local time. Submit your project to receive a participant certificate and to be eligible for Global Judging. We encourage all participants to submit what they accomplish over the course of the hackathon.
[Learn about Project Submission](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/)
Step 06

## Complete the Participant Survey
December 1, 2026
Share your feedback about your hackathon journey to help improve future events.
